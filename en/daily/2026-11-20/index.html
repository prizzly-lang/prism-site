Light puzzles for Friday, November 20, 2026

Small shapes stand for colors of light: ▲ is red, ■ is green, ● is blue. Two shapes together mean the two colors mixed.

Boxes are light sources. A white box sends light of the color shown inside it; the solid black box sends white light. Light leaves through the pointed side of the box.

A crystal (hexagon) lights up only when all the light reaching it adds up to exactly the color shown inside it. Any extra color keeps it dark.

A plate with gray between two lines is a beam splitter. Light goes straight through and is reflected like a mirror at the same time, so it splits in two.

A striped circle is a polarizer. Unpolarized light passes and comes out polarized along the stripes; light that is already polarized passes only if it matches the stripes. A source with gray stripes on its box sends light that is already polarized.

Lines show where light travels: white is a solid line, red long dashes, green dash-dot, blue dots.

Answers for Friday, November 20, 2026

A black circle with a number shows how many times to tap that piece. The lines show where the light goes once the board is solved. On large boards where numbers crowd together, use the list under each picture.
