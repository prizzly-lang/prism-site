Thirty seconds and you know the game

There is only one rule

Tap a piece and it turns 90°; the light finds a new path instantly. Light every crystal with exactly the color it asks for, all at once, and the board is solved. That is the only rule, and there is no manual. The levels teach it.

  • 300 puzzles across seven chapters
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution
  • The par shown is the true minimum, found by exhaustive search
  • No ads · no subscription · no sign-up
  • No networking except to make a purchase

Seven chapters

The levels teach optics in order. Each chapter introduces exactly one new piece.

  1. IReflecta mirror turns light by 90 degrees
  2. IISplita half-silvered mirror passes light and reflects it at the same time
  3. IIIDispersea prism splits white light into red, green and blue
  4. IVMixtwo beams meeting at one crystal make a new color
  5. VRefinea filter keeps only the component you want
  6. VIConvergeeverything you have learned meets on one board
  7. VIIPolarizecrossed polarizers make light disappear

Real optics

The angle of reflection, dispersion through a prism, additive color mixing, and crossed polarizers blocking light are real physics. Each chapter opens a principle card that shows the board before and after a single tap, side by side, written so a child can follow it. Where the game simplifies real physics, we say so.

Playable without seeing color

Every crystal also shows its required color as a shape, and mixed colors show their component shapes side by side: a yellow crystal carries a triangle and a square together. All 300 puzzles can be finished without distinguishing color.

  • red = triangle
  • green = square
  • blue = circle
  • Text size can be set to normal, large or extra large
  • Turning on Reduce Motion cuts down the effects
  • A tap that misses a piece by a little still rotates the nearest one

Four languages

Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese. Follows your device language, or pick one in Settings.

Questions people ask

Are there really no ads?

None. No subscription, no sign-up, no sign-in. On iPhone and iPad it is free to download: play chapter 1 (14 levels), and one purchase opens the other 286. On Android you pay once when you get it, and all 300 are open from the start. If you were looking for a puzzle game with no ads, that is why this one exists.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. It only goes online to make a purchase. It works on a plane, underground, anywhere with no signal. Your progress is stored only on this device and is never sent anywhere.

Can I play if I am colorblind?

Yes. Every crystal carries its required color as a shape as well (red is a triangle, green a square, blue a circle), so all 300 puzzles can be finished without distinguishing color at all.

How many levels, and how hard?

Seven chapters, 300 boards. Every board has exactly one solution, so nothing is solved by luck, and if you are stuck there is always a reason. The par shown is the true minimum, proven by exhaustive search.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. Same 300 puzzles, and either way you pay only once: on iOS it is free to download and unlocked in the app, on Android you pay when you get it.

How big is it?

Under 5 MB. There is not a single image file in the app. Every beam and shape is drawn in code.

Can I use it to teach?

Yes. The seven chapters cover reflection, beam splitting, dispersion, additive color mixing, color filters, spectroscopy and polarization, and each opens with a card on the real physics and what the game simplifies. A teacher's guide on this site lists every chapter's principle with a question to ask students.
