Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

PRISM does not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. PRISM does not:

  • ask for or store any personally identifying information such as your name, email address or phone number
  • require a sign-up or sign-in
  • read the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android)
  • include analytics that collect usage, play patterns or events
  • include crash-reporting tools
  • access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera
  • use cookies or similar tracking technologies

Networking

On iOS, PRISM contacts no server except the App Store (Apple StoreKit) when you make a purchase. The Android app contacts no server at all. The game itself works with no internet connection.

What is stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • your best tap count for each level
  • your sound, haptics, language and text size settings
  • which hints (such as principle cards) you have already seen, and whether the store rating prompt was already shown
  • (iOS) whether the game is unlocked (you paid once, or you got it while it was a paid app)
  • (Android) a marker that a paid copy was installed and run on this device

This is kept in the standard app storage on your device (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android) and is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. It may be included in iCloud or Android device backups; those are Apple's and Google's backup features, not a transfer by PRISM.

The web demo and this website

The browser demo (prism.adriven.co/play/) keeps the same progress and settings in your browser's storage (localStorage), in that browser only. It is never sent to a server, and clearing the browser's site data removes it.

This website and the demo have no analytics or advertising scripts and no cookies. The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process technical logs such as visitors' IP addresses to operate and secure the service, under GitHub's privacy statement. PRISM and A Driven do not receive these logs.

Third parties

There is no data to share, so nothing is shared with third parties. PRISM has no third-party SDK that collects data. It uses an open-source plugin (@capgo/native-purchases) that calls Apple StoreKit for purchases and makes no network requests of its own.

Payment

On iOS, PRISM is free to download and one purchase (a non-consumable in-app purchase) opens everything after chapter 1. Purchases go through the App Store and are handled by Apple. PRISM never sees payment information such as your card number, name or Apple ID; the app records only whether it is unlocked, on the device. If you got PRISM while it was a paid app (through version 1.5), the app recognises this on the device from the app transaction Apple provides (the version you first downloaded) and unlocks automatically. That is not sent anywhere either.

On Android, PRISM is a one-time paid app. The purchase goes through Google Play and is handled by Google. The Android app has no in-app purchases.

Children

PRISM is for all ages (4+). Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children. It performs no processing that would require parental consent under COPPA or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or kept, there is no data to access, correct, delete or restrict. To clear the progress stored on your device, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change in the app's release notes. There are no plans to start collecting data.
