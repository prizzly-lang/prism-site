Common questions

I cannot solve a puzzle. Is the level impossible?

No. Every one of the 300 levels was machine-proven to have exactly one solution before it shipped. The minimum tap count shown is not an estimate but the true minimum found by exhaustive search. So that you never get stuck on one level, open levels do not have to be cleared in order: you can go straight to the next puzzle from the level list.

Light reaches a crystal but it does not turn on.

A crystal lights only for an exact colour match. If a crystal that wants red receives red and green together, that makes yellow and it fails. Check whether stray light is mixing in. This is the rule, not a bug.

Colours are hard for me to tell apart.

Every crystal also shows its required colour as a shape: red is a triangle, green a square, blue a circle. A mixed colour such as yellow shows a triangle and a square side by side. All 300 puzzles can be finished without seeing colour at all. If something could be better, please tell us; accessibility suggestions come first.

My progress is gone.

Progress is stored only on your device and is not backed up to any server (there is no sign-up, and the app goes online only to make a purchase). Deleting the app deletes your progress. When you change devices, it may move across with an iCloud device backup (iPhone and iPad) or an Android device backup.

I already bought it, but levels are locked. (iPhone and iPad)

If you got PRISM while it was a paid app (through version 1.5), it unlocks by itself. If you paid on another device, tap Restore Purchase in Settings. If it is still locked, email your receipt to prism@adriven.co. On Android you pay when you get the app, so all 300 levels are open from the start.

Will there be ads or a subscription?

No. There will be no ads and no subscription. One payment is the whole deal (on iOS an in-app purchase that opens everything after chapter 1, on Android the price of the app).

I would like a refund.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Refunds for Google Play purchases follow Google Play's refund policy; see Google Play Help on refunds, and email prism@adriven.co if you need help.
