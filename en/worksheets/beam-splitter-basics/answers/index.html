Splitting Light in Two

1One beam splitter

Turn the mirror in D4 once. Leave D1 and D3 alone.

The light going right from A1 bends down at D1 and splits at the beam splitter in D3. The beam that bends right reaches the crystal at E3. The beam that goes straight down bends right at D4 and reaches the crystal at E4.

2Two beams that meet

Turn the mirror in A5 once. Leave A4, C5 and D4 alone.

The light coming down from A1 splits at the beam splitter in A4. The beam that bends right passes B4 and C4, bends up at D4 and reaches the crystal at D1. The beam that goes straight down bends right at A5 and up at C5, passes C4 (crossing the first beam) and reaches the crystal at C1.

3Only one crystal is lit

Turn the mirrors in A3 and B4 once each. Leave the beam splitter in B3 alone.

The light coming down from B1 splits at the beam splitter in B3. The beam that bends left bends up at A3 and reaches the crystal at A2. The beam that goes straight down bends left at B4 and reaches the crystal at A4.

4Two beam splitters

Turn the mirror in A3 and the beam splitters in B2 and B4 once each. Leave the mirror in A2 alone.

The light going left from E3 bends up at A3 and right at A2, then splits at the beam splitter in B2. The beam that goes straight passes C2, D2 and E2 and leaves the board. The beam that bends down crosses the first beam in B3 and splits again at the beam splitter in B4: the beam that bends left reaches the crystal at A4, and the beam that goes straight down reaches the crystal at B5.

Back page answers

  1. One beam bends right and reaches the crystal at E3. The other goes straight down to the mirror in D4.
  2. The crystal at A4. On the solved board it is lit by the beam that goes straight down from the beam splitter (B3) and bends at the mirror in B4. The beam that bends left, after the mirror in A3 is turned, lights the crystal at A2.
  3. In C4, which is an empty cell with no mirror. With a mirror there, both beams passing through would have bent in that cell and gone somewhere else.
  4. Three beams. Two reach a crystal (A4 and B5). The third is the beam that goes straight on at the beam splitter in B2: it passes C2, D2 and E2 and leaves the board.
