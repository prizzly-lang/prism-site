Steering Light with Mirrors: a printable reflection worksheet for beginners

A worksheet built from four of the easiest boards in the game, with mirrors and nothing else. It has two A4 pages (front and back) for students and a one-page answer key for teachers. On the front, students turn mirrors to send light to a crystal. On the back, short questions check two ideas: light travels in straight lines, and a mirror changes its direction.

It works on a black and white printer: every color of light is also marked with a shape (green , blue ).

For ages about 9 to 12, after a first lesson on how light travels and reflects. Connects to NGSS 4-PS4-2 (US) and KS2 Light (England). One “Going further” box on the back introduces the law of reflection.

Straight lines and reflection

How to play

  1. Light leaves the source (the square box) in the direction of its small arrow. It keeps going straight until it meets something.
  2. Each tap turns a mirror 90 degrees, so it swaps between a “/” shape and a “\” shape. Crystals and sources never move.
  3. The board is solved when the crystal (hexagon) receives light of the color drawn inside it.

Follow the light with your eyes first. Then circle the mirrors you would turn and draw the path in pencil.

Light goes straight, then bends at a mirror

1Light travels in straight lines

Switch on a flashlight and the beam does not curve. It goes straight ahead, which is why a shadow forms behind an object. On the boards, light also runs in one straight line until it meets a mirror or a crystal.

On board 1, where did the light go before you turned a mirror? Describe its path in words.

2A mirror changes the direction of light

When light hits a mirror it bounces off and heads a different way. This is called reflection. On the boards, the way a mirror leans decides where it sends the light.

Going further A line drawn at right angles to a mirror is called the normal. The angle between the incoming light and the normal always equals the angle between the outgoing light and the normal. The two angles marked in the picture above are the same size.

Which way does the light go next? Write “up” or “down”.

3Count the bends

Every time the light meets a mirror, its path bends once.

On the solved boards, how many times did the light bend before it reached the crystal?

On the solved board 3, where would the light go if you turned the A3 mirror once?

4Crossing beams carry on

Shine two flashlights so that their beams cross. The beams do not bump into each other. Each one carries on along its own path.

On board 4, the light crosses its own earlier path once. In which cell? Did the light change direction there?
