Mirror Board Challenge

Light in the answer diagrams

  • red
  • green
  • 1number of taps

1Two sources, three mirrors

Turn B3, C3 and D3 once each.

The light coming down from C1 bends right at C3 and down at D3, reaching the crystal at D5. The light going up from B5 bends left at B3 and reaches the crystal at A3.

2Beams that cross

Turn C3, D2 and D4 once each.

The light going right from A2 passes C2, bends down at D2 and right at D4, reaching the crystal at E4. The light coming down from C1 crosses the first beam at C2, then bends left at C3 and reaches the crystal at B3.

3Around the square

Turn B2, B3, D2 and D3 once each.

The light going up from D5 bends left at D3, up at B3, right at B2 and up again at D2, reaching the crystal at D1.

4Two sources, one crystal

Turn A4, B3, B4 and C3 once each.

The light coming down from C1 bends left at C3, down at B3, left at B4 and up at A4, reaching the crystal at A1. The light from the source at E1 ends at the source at C1.

Back page answers

  1. No mirror starts out the right way. Turn all three once: three turns in all.
  2. D2, B2, B3, D3, in that order. At the end you reach the source at D5.
  3. In C2, which is an empty cell with no mirror. With no mirror, both beams carry straight on. With a mirror there, that beam would have bent in C2.
  4. It passes D1 and ends at the source in C1. The light that lights the crystal comes from the source in C1 and meets four mirrors (C3, B3, B4, A4).
