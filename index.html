색을 구분하기 어려운 분들을 위해

모든 결정에는 요구하는 색이 도형으로 함께 각인되어 있습니다. 빨강은 삼각형, 초록은 사각형, 파랑은 원입니다. 노란 결정에는 삼각형과 사각형이 나란히 새겨져 있습니다. 색을 전혀 보지 못해도 240개 퍼즐 전부를 풀 수 있습니다.

PRISM (English)

Tap a piece and it turns 90°; the light finds a new path instantly. Light every crystal with exactly the color it asks for, all at once, and the board is solved. That is the only rule, and there is no manual — the levels teach it.

  • 240 puzzles across six chapters
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution
  • No ads · no in-app purchases · no account
  • No networking at all. Fully offline
  • Completable without seeing color

Every crystal also shows its required color as a shape: red is a triangle, green a square, blue a circle. A yellow crystal shows a triangle and a square side by side. All 240 puzzles can be finished without distinguishing color.
